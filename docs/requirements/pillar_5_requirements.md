# Requirements Specification: Pillar 5 — Capacity Building for the Official Statistical System

## 1. Problem Statement Clause
> *"...to strengthen capacity building in India's Official Statistical System."*

---

## 2. Theoretical & Conceptual Definition

### A. The Institutional Ecosystem: MoSPI & the Indian Statistical System
The target beneficiary of this platform is not general academia or commercial IT workers; it is specifically the **Official Statistical System of India**, governed by the **Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI)**, the **National Statistical Commission (NSC)**, and state **Directorates of Economics & Statistics (DES)**.

This system relies on specialized sub-organizations, each with distinct operational mandates:
1. **NSSO (National Sample Survey Office)**:
   * **SDRD (Survey Design & Research Division)**: Responsible for sampling methodology, questionnaire formulation, and concept definitions.
   * **FOD (Field Operations Division)**: Responsible for ground-level data collection across thousands of rural/urban First Stage Units (FSUs) using CAPI (Computer-Assisted Personal Interviewing).
   * **DPD (Data Processing Division)**: Responsible for data validation, imputation, tabulations, and micro-data generation.
2. **CSO (Central Statistics Office)**:
   * **NAD (National Accounts Division)**: Responsible for compiling Gross Domestic Product (GDP), Gross Value Added (GVA), and National Income aggregates.
   * **Price Statistics Division**: Responsible for compiling monthly Consumer Price Index (CPI) and inflation statistics.
3. **Legal & Compliance Infrastructure**:
   * **Collection of Statistics Act, 2008**: Legal statutory powers and penalties governing official data gathering.
   * **Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA), 2023**: Mandatory legal compliance for data fiduciaries handling citizen survey data.

---

### B. Defining Institutional Capacity Building
In the civil service, **Capacity Building** moves beyond individual learning to institutional capability. It entails:
1. **Macro Diagnostic Visibility**: Providing leadership with real-time aggregated visibility into systemic knowledge deficits across divisions and field directorates.
2. **Targeted Interventions**: Eliminating institutional blind spots (e.g., if 65% of field investigators in a specific regional office demonstrate poor comprehension of rural hamlet-group selection rules, training resources can be targeted immediately).
3. **Standardization of Official Methodology**: Ensuring that official statistical standards, classifications (e.g., NIC - National Industrial Classification), and survey definitions are executed uniformly across all 36 States and Union Territories.

---

## 3. Evaluator & Ministry Expectations

When evaluated by MoSPI senior leadership, Capacity Building Commission (CBC) representatives, or SIH jury members, Pillar 5 must satisfy four explicit criteria:

### 1. Domain Fidelity & Cadre Grounding
* The platform must demonstrate authentic statistical rigor. Questions, competencies, and terminology must reflect real Indian statistical processes:
  * Concepts: *First Stage Units (FSU)*, *Ultimate Sampling Units (USU)*, *Laspeyres/Paasche formulations*, *Multiplier Estimation*, *CAPI sync protocols*, *DPDPA Data Fiduciary compliance*.
  * Evaluators will immediately penalize generic, commercial LMS templates with superficial statistical labels.

### 2. Department-Wide Macro Analytics (The Leadership View)
* In addition to the individual officer's view, the platform must provide an administrative/departmental dashboard reflecting aggregated institutional health:
  * Total assessments completed across the department.
  * System-wide average FRAC competency scores.
  * Identification of the **Top Institutional Competency Gap** requiring urgent ministerial training intervention.

### 3. Division & Regional Granularity
* Evaluators expect the capacity view to segment data across operational branches (e.g., comparing proficiency between the *Field Operations Division* and the *National Accounts Division*).

### 4. Policy & Audit Reporting (The Capacity Building Dossier)
* The platform must provide exportable documentation (e.g., a formal PDF/CSV Institutional Competency Dossier) that can be submitted to the Capacity Building Commission (CBC) for annual training audits.
